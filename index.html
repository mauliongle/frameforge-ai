FAQ

Questions?
We've Got Answers.

What makes FrameForge AI different from other video editors?
FrameForge AI is built from the ground up with AI at its core — not as an afterthought. Our AI understands your content contextually, making intelligent editing decisions that would take hours manually. From auto-detecting the best moments for clips to matching cinematic color grades, every feature is designed to save time while delivering professional results.
Do I need video editing experience?
Not at all! FrameForge AI is designed for everyone — from complete beginners to professional editors. The AI handles the technical complexity while you focus on your creative vision. Beginners can let AI do everything, while pros can fine-tune every detail.
What video formats are supported?
FrameForge AI supports all major video formats including MP4, MOV, AVI, MKV, WebM, and more. You can export in any format with custom resolution, bitrate, and codec settings. We also offer optimized presets for every major platform.
Is my footage processed locally or in the cloud?
FrameForge AI uses a hybrid approach. Light processing and preview rendering happen locally for speed, while heavy AI tasks (like clip generation and advanced color grading) leverage our cloud infrastructure for maximum performance. Your footage is encrypted end-to-end and never stored permanently on our servers.
Can I cancel my subscription anytime?
Absolutely. No contracts, no commitments. You can cancel your subscription at any time and continue using FrameForge AI until the end of your billing period. Your projects and exports are always yours to keep.
